ABOUT CURELEE

One place for a more connected healthcare experience.

Curelee is designed to make communication and healthcare management easier for patients and doctors. Patients can discover nearby doctors, check availability, connect with the appropriate healthcare team and manage appointments.

Doctors can access relevant patient information, history, prescriptions and reports according to the features and permissions provided by the app.
